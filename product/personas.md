# Personas

Who we serve. Use in proposals, UI copy, and scope decisions.
Related: [brief-saas.md](brief-saas.md) · [brief-msp.md](brief-msp.md) · [../plans/gtm.md](../plans/gtm.md)

---

## 1. Pastor / ministry lead (“Shepherd”)

**Context:** Accountable for mission and people; tech is a means, not the calling. Time-poor. Risk-averse about anything that could disrupt Sunday.

**Pains:** Live stream or giving fails during service; no clear owner for IT; vendors speak jargon; fear of looking incompetent to the board.

**Wins:** Quiet reliability; one calm partner; clear pricing; someone who understands that Sunday morning is sacred time.

**ChapelCloud fit:** SaaS that “just works,” MSP retainer with Sunday severity tiers, training for volunteers so the pastor is not the helpdesk.

**Watch-outs:** Do not oversell features. Do not schedule invasive changes on Saturday night without consent.

---

## 2. Church administrator / ops lead (“Steward”)

**Context:** Runs calendars, facilities, vendors, sometimes bookkeeping. Often the real buyer.

**Pains:** Tool sprawl; unclear invoices; volunteers changing configs; compliance/insurance questions about cameras and data.

**Wins:** Consolidated billing story; documented runbooks; predictable retainers; audit-friendly access logs.

**ChapelCloud fit:** Hosting + giving integration, bookkeeping guidance, MSP documentation, security system design with clear limits.

---

## 3. Tech volunteer (“Tent peg”)

**Context:** Gifted, unpaid or lightly paid, holds tribal knowledge. Proud of the setup; afraid of being replaced or blamed.

**Pains:** On-call every Sunday; no budget; gear held together with hope; no backup person.

**Wins:** Respect; training that elevates them; diagrams; remote backup brain when they are on vacation.

**ChapelCloud fit:** We train and partner — we do not humiliate DIY work. MSP + training is the love language. Later hardware should feel like an upgrade they helped choose.

**Watch-outs:** Never dunk on their past choices in front of leadership.

---

## 4. Finance steward / treasurer (“Ledger”)

**Context:** Fiduciary care for offerings and restricted funds. Cares about fees, statements, and integrity.

**Pains:** Opaque processing fees; reconciliation pain; tools that do not understand funds; fear of impropriety.

**Wins:** Transparent fee structures; clean exports; clear separation of duties; partners who take Prov 11:1 seriously.

**ChapelCloud fit:** Giving integrations with honest fee talk; bookkeeping integrations; custom pricing that is written down.

---

## 5. Nonprofit ops lead (Christian or other) (“Builder”)

**Context:** Similar to church admin but often grant-driven, fewer Sunday constraints, still budget-tight.

**Pains:** Website neglect; donation UX; no IT staff.

**Wins:** Fast credible web presence; giving that works on mobile; optional light MSP.

**ChapelCloud fit:** Year-1 wedge customer (website + donations). Primary focus remains God-focused orgs; other nonprofits welcome when aligned.

---

## 6. Anti-persona (politely decline or defer)

- Buyers who require immediate nationwide on-site reno on our dime (year 1)
- Orgs wanting us to hide Christian identity or, conversely, to perform aggressive culture-war branding
- Customers who demand 24/7 heroics for non-critical tickets with no retainer
- Projects that need PCI scope we are not ready to hold in-house (use processors)

---

## Sizing note

We do **not** filter by attendance. Pricing and scope flex so small churches can participate and larger churches sustain outreach. Persona fit matters more than headcount.
