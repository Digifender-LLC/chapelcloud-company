# Brief — SaaS

Year-1 product brief for ChapelCloud software offers.
Related: [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) · [personas.md](personas.md) · [../plans/business-plan.md](../plans/business-plan.md)

---

## Intent

Provide cloud tools and **integrations** that churches and ministries actually need — starting with hosting and giving — without attempting full ChMS parity with Tithely, Pushpay, Planning Center, or Subsplash in year 1.

---

## In scope (year 1)

| Offer | Approach | Success metric |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Website hosting | Managed hosting (Cloudflare Pages / adjacent stack); uptime and stewardship support | First nonprofit site live |
| Giving / donations | Integrate reputable processors / giving platforms; we configure, brand, train | Donations flowing; admin can operate |
| Live streaming & restream | Restream to church site and social platforms; integrate proven streaming partners | Live + social destinations working; team can operate |
| Long-term video storage | Durable sermon/event archives with clear stewardship and access | Archives usable by staff; retention policy documented |
| Podcast services | Hosting, feeds, and handoff for weekly teaching audio | Feed live; admin training done |
| Transcription | Transcripts for accessibility, study, and search | Transcripts available for agreed services |
| Bookkeeping | Advise + integrate fund-aware tools; do not build a full ledger yet | Clear recommendation path documented |
| Integrations catalog | Written list of preferred tools and when we use them | Catalog exists and used in proposals |
| Platform scaffolding | Orgs, auth, billing hooks, device registry (for future MSP/hardware stickiness) | Scaffold advances only as needed for delivery |

## Out of scope (year 1)

- Full church management suite (membership, check-in, volunteer apps as owned products)
- Building a proprietary payment processor
- Mobile apps for congregants
- Feature races with national ChMS brands

---

## Principles

- **Integrate first, build second** — count the cost (Luke 14:28)
- **Just weights** — fee transparency for giving partners (Prov 11:1)
- **Mission-first UX** — calm, clear; Christian without cult vibe
- **Flywheel** — every SaaS engagement invites a soft MSP discovery when appropriate

---

## Near-term delivery: waiting nonprofit

1. Domain / DNS / hosting  
2. Site content path (template or existing)  
3. Donation flow via partner  
4. Admin training + recorded notes  
5. Optional intro to remote IT assessment  

---

## Dependencies

- Entity + invoicing before paid retainers  
- PCI: remain out of cardholder data scope where possible (hosted fields / partner hosted checkout)  
- Brand and home-site CTAs already point to hello@chapel.cloud  

---

## Open questions (resolve in delivery, not blockers)

- Preferred giving partner(s) for default proposals  
- Whether bookkeeping stays advisory-only through all of 2026  
- When portal login becomes customer-facing vs email-operated
