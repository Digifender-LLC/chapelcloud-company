# Brief — Managed IT (MSP)

Year-1 product brief for ChapelCloud remote church MSP.
Related: [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) · [personas.md](personas.md) · [../ops/vendors.md](../ops/vendors.md)

---

## Intent

Be the remote technology partner churches trust for networks, security systems, monitoring, and **volunteer training** — including Sunday service-hour support — without travel or full campus renovations in year 1.

---

## In scope (year 1)

| Service | Notes |
|---------|--------|
| Remote assessment | Questionnaire, video walkthrough, config review |
| Network design | Wi‑Fi, switching, firewall, segmentation (guest / staff / AV) |
| Security systems | Cameras, access control, alarms — design, remote config/management where platform allows |
| Monitoring & patching | RMM/MDM when cost fits; backup guidance |
| Runbooks | Written procedures for volunteers and staff |
| Training | Live remote + recorded sessions |
| Incident response | Severity-tiered; 7-day including Sunday service hours |
| Vendor selection | Whitelabel → OSS → Ubiquiti (flex by size/budget) |

## Out of scope (year 1)

- Travel and on-site installation labor  
- Full campus “rip and replace” renovations  
- Guaranteeing physical security outcomes we cannot verify on-site  
- Absorbing hardware inventory risk for non-customers  

On-site needs → defer to year 2+, or customer-hired local labor following our design.

---

## Packaging (suggested)

| Package | Includes (illustrative) |
|---------|-------------------------|
| Assessment only | One-time remote findings + roadmap |
| Care retainer | Monitoring, remote changes, training hours, Sunday P1 coverage |
| Security add-on | Camera/access/alarm platform stewardship |
| Project (remote) | Firewall cutover, Wi‑Fi redesign implemented remotely |

Exact SKUs and prices: market-rate starting points + custom ministry quotes ([business-plan](../plans/business-plan.md)).

---

## Sunday support model

| Severity | Example | Response intent |
|----------|---------|-----------------|
| P1 — worship-blocking | Stream down, payment terminals offline, sanctuary Wi‑Fi dead | On-call engages during service hours |
| P2 — degraded | Single AP issue, non-critical camera offline | Same day / next business window |
| P3 — backlog | New VLAN request, training scheduling | Normal queue |

Family rotation and limits: [../people/README.md](../people/README.md).

---

## Success metrics

- First paid remote MSP retainer  
- Documented assessment template reused  
- At least one volunteer training delivered and rated helpful  
- No travel exceptions that break year-1 capital discipline  

---

## Flywheel

MSP delight → invite hosting/giving consolidation. SaaS delight → invite assessment. Never pressure; always steward.
