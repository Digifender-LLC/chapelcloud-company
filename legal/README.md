# Legal

Drafts, checklists, and pointers only.

Do not commit executed contracts with sensitive counterparty data, EINs, or banking details. Link to the vault or counsel folder instead.

Related: [../plans/business-plan.md](../plans/business-plan.md) · [../decisions/0002-christian-operating-principles.md](../decisions/0002-christian-operating-principles.md)

## Stage

**Pre-formation.** Form the entity when the first paid invoice is imminent. Until then, avoid taking on liability-heavy work (especially physical security systems) without clear written limits.

---

## Pre-formation checklist

### Entity and tax

- [ ] Choose entity type (likely LLC) and state of formation
- [ ] File formation documents
- [ ] EIN
- [ ] Business bank account (no mixing with personal once operating)
- [ ] Sales tax / nexus review if selling hardware later
- [ ] Accountant relationship for nonprofit/church invoicing norms

### Insurance

- [ ] General liability
- [ ] Professional / errors & omissions (remote IT advisory)
- [ ] Cyber liability (when holding customer credentials or hosting)
- [ ] Review coverage before camera / access-control contracts
- [ ] Workers’ comp when hiring

### Contracts (templates in vault, not git)

- [ ] Master services agreement (MSA)
- [ ] Statement of work (SOW) / change order
- [ ] Acceptable use / support severity attachment (Sunday P1 defined)
- [ ] Data processing / confidentiality terms
- [ ] Hardware sales / limited warranty (year 2+)
- [ ] Subprocessor list for hosting and giving partners

### Payments and giving

- [ ] Prefer processors that keep ChapelCloud **out of PCI cardholder data scope**
- [ ] Document fee responsibility (church vs ChapelCloud vs processor)
- [ ] Refund and chargeback escalation path

### Privacy and stewardship

- [ ] Data retention defaults (church owns their data)
- [ ] Offboarding / export process
- [ ] Credential handling (password manager; no shared spreadsheets)
- [ ] Camera / access systems: clarify monitoring expectations and privacy law sensitivity (especially areas with higher expectation of privacy)

### Brand and web

- [ ] Domain chapel.cloud registrant and renewals documented in [../ops/accounts.md](../ops/accounts.md)
- [ ] Trademark watch (informal OK at start; formal filing when revenue justifies)

---

## Counsel

When revenue or risk warrants it, use a lawyer familiar with MSPs and/or nonprofit customers. Until then, use reputable templates and still read what you sign.

---

## Non-negotiables (ethics)

Aligned with ADR-0002: honest pricing, no bait-and-switch, clear scope, no promises of on-site work we cannot fund in year 1.
