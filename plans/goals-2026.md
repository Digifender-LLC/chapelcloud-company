# Goals — 2026 (year 1)

Internal milestones for ChapelCloud’s pre-formation / bootstrap year.
Related: [business-plan.md](business-plan.md) · [gtm.md](gtm.md) · [../product/roadmap.md](../product/roadmap.md)

**North star:** Become a known, desirable partner in the church technology community; cover operating costs; start the SaaS ⇄ MSP flywheel. No fixed revenue target.

---

## Outcomes (must achieve)

| Outcome | Definition of done |
|---------|-------------------|
| Reputation | At least one external referral or warm intro originating from delivered work or trusted relationships |
| Cost coverage | Domain, email, Cloudflare, and essential tools paid without personal crisis accounting |
| First SaaS win | Waiting nonprofit (or equivalent) live on hosting + donations with documented handoff |
| First MSP retainer | At least one remote managed agreement (monthly) — can be light scope |
| Faithful ops | Entity + insurance + MSA path ready before material liability; Christian operating principles followed |

---

## Milestone checklist

### Foundation

- [ ] Confirm password manager / secrets process (nothing sensitive in git)
- [ ] Form legal entity when first paid invoice is imminent
- [ ] Open business banking + invoicing method
- [ ] Liability insurance appropriate for remote IT / advisory (upgrade before security system contracts)
- [ ] MSA + SOW templates reviewed (even if DIY counsel checklist first)
- [ ] Accept [ADR-0002 Christian operating principles](../decisions/0002-christian-operating-principles.md) in practice

### Product & delivery

- [ ] Integration catalog draft (giving, hosting, bookkeeping partners)
- [ ] Remote MSP assessment questionnaire + deliverable template
- [ ] Support severity tiers + Sunday on-call rotation written ([people](../people/README.md))
- [ ] Vendor ladder documented ([ops/vendors.md](../ops/vendors.md))
- [ ] Platform scaffold remains intentional — no premature ChMS build
- [ ] Hardware: research notes only; no inventory speculation

### Go-to-market

- [ ] Delight first nonprofit website + donations customer
- [ ] Ask for one peer introduction after success
- [ ] Publish 2–4 practical stewardship articles or posts
- [ ] Home-site MSP section planned ([home-site-msp-brief.md](home-site-msp-brief.md)); implement when capacity allows
- [ ] Reconnect (gently) with prior volunteer-church relationships

### Flywheel

- [ ] At least one SaaS → MSP or MSP → SaaS cross-sell conversation that is welcomed (not forced)
- [ ] Case notes / testimonial permission captured where appropriate

---

## Quarterly rhythm (suggested)

| Quarter | Focus |
|---------|--------|
| Q1 mindset (whenever year starts) | Entity readiness, first SaaS delivery, support model |
| Next | Remote MSP packaging, first retainer, content |
| Later in year | Site MSP update, integration catalog polish, year-2 travel/reno criteria |

Adjust dates when the calendar year and formation date are firm; keep the sequence.

---

## Explicit non-goals (2026)

- Travel-funded installs or full campus renovations  
- Raising outside capital as a goal (optional later, not required)  
- Feature parity with Tithely / Pushpay / Planning Center / Subsplash  
- Stocking large hardware inventory  
- Hiring before recurring revenue covers the role
