## Why

The live site today markets **Software** (giving, hosting, bookkeeping) and **Hardware** (NDI, e-paper). Year-1 differentiation also includes **remote Managed IT, security systems, and volunteer training**. The site should say so without promising travel or campus renovations.

---

## Voice

- Mission-first, clear, calm  
- Christian identity **evident** (stewardship, serving the church) — not cultish or shouty  
- Brand name **ChapelCloud** remains the hero-level signal on the first viewport; do not clutter the hero with MSP stats or service grids  

---

## Suggested information architecture

Keep existing hero. Add a third primary section (or rename nav):

1. Software (existing)  
2. **Care** or **Managed IT** (new) — prefer plain language over “MSP” in headlines  
3. Hardware (existing; keep “later” honesty if needed)  

Nav: Software · Care · Hardware · Talk with us

## Section copy direction (draft)

**Eyebrow:** Care  

**Headline:** Remote technology care for ministry rhythms  

**Support:** Networks, security, and volunteer training — including Sunday service hours — so your people can focus on the mission.  

**Bullets / rows (not cards in hero; simple list like Software section):**

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Network stewardship | Wi‑Fi, firewalls, and segmentation designed for guest, staff, and production — assessed and managed remotely. |
| Security systems | Cameras, access control, and alarms planned with privacy and insurance in mind, managed from afar where the platform allows. |
| Volunteer training | Runbooks and live remote training so tent-makers are supported, not stranded. |
| Sunday-aware support | Severity-tiered help when worship-critical systems fail — including service hours. |

**Honesty line (one short sentence):** On-site campus renovations and travel projects come later; year one we serve you remotely with excellence.
