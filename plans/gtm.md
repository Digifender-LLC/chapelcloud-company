# Go-to-market (year 1)

Internal playbook. Remote-first. Zero travel budget.
Related: [business-plan.md](business-plan.md) · [goals-2026.md](goals-2026.md) · [home-site-msp-brief.md](home-site-msp-brief.md)

---

## 1. Objective

Make ChapelCloud a **known and desirable** name in church technology circles — trusted enough that pastors, admins, and tech volunteers recommend us — while covering operating costs through SaaS first, then remote MSP.

We are not optimizing for vanity metrics or paid acquisition we cannot afford.

---

## 2. Positioning

**One-liner (internal):** Christian-operated SaaS and remote church MSP — technology that serves the mission, including Sundays.

**Public tone:** Calm, clear, mission-first. Explicitly Christian without cult aesthetics. Non-believers should feel welcome and respected.

**Do not claim year 1:** nationwide on-site reno crews, full ChMS parity with Tithely/Pushpay, or “we’ll fly out tomorrow.”

**Do claim year 1:** hosting and giving stewardship, integrations, remote network/security design and management, volunteer training, Sunday-aware support.

---

## 3. Target segments (priority order)

1. **God-focused churches** of any size with a clear tech pain (site down, giving friction, fragile Wi‑Fi, no Sunday support)
2. **Ministries / Christian nonprofits** (same stack)
3. **Other nonprofits** when ethics and budget fit
4. **Warm lead:** one nonprofit already asking for website + donations — close and delight this first

Personas: [../product/personas.md](../product/personas.md)

---

## 4. Channels (zero/low cost)

| Channel | How we use it | Year-1 expect |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Direct delivery excellence | First customer becomes a story and referral source | Primary |
| Personal network | Prior volunteer church relationships; ask for introductions, not hard sells | Primary |
| Referrals | Simple “know a church that needs…?” ask after good outcomes | Primary |
| Content | Short practical guides (Sunday Wi‑Fi, guest network, giving setup) on site/blog or LinkedIn | Secondary |
| Church associations / pastor groups | Participate helpfully; no spam | Secondary |
| Paid ads | Avoid until revenue covers them | Out |

### Content themes (stewardship, not hype)

- Segmenting church Wi‑Fi (guest / staff / AV)
- What to do when the stream dies mid-service
- Choosing a giving tool without regret
- Camera and access control questions boards should ask
- Training volunteers without burning them out

Voice matches [brand guidelines](../../brand/guidelines/README.md).

## 5. Offer packaging for conversations

Lead with the pain they named, then map:

| They say | We open with |
|----------|----------------|
| “We need a site / online giving” | SaaS hosting + giving integration |
| “Volunteers keep the lights on and it’s fragile” | Remote MSP + training |
| “Cameras / doors / alarms” | Security design + remote management |
| “Everything is a mess” | Discovery call → phased SaaS then MSP (not full reno year 1) |

Always leave a **flywheel next step** (SaaS → MSP or MSP → SaaS) without pressure.

---

## 6. Sales motion (remote)

1. **Intake** — email / form / hello@chapel.cloud; short questionnaire  
2. **Discovery call** (video) — mission, budget band, tools, Sunday constraints  
3. **Written proposal** — scope, starting-at rate, custom quote, what’s out of scope (travel/reno)  
4. **MSA / SOW** — after entity formation for paid work  
5. **Kickoff** — access, change windows, support severity explanation  
6. **30-day check-in** — ask for referral if delighted  

### Pricing in the room

- Share market-rate anchors  
- Customize for ministry budget  
- If subsidizing a small church, note it internally and keep the quote clear to the customer (just weights)  
- Larger churches: closer to list; explain value of retainers and Sunday coverage  

---

## 7. First-customer play (waiting nonprofit)

**Goal:** Website + donations live, calm support experience, written case notes (with permission).

Checklist:

- [ ] Clarify brand, domain, content ownership  
- [ ] Hosting plan on ChapelCloud-managed stack  
- [ ] Giving partner selected and integrated (PCI stays with processor)  
- [ ] Admin training session recorded  
- [ ] Invite soft conversation about any IT/security needs (flywheel)  
- [ ] Ask for introduction to one peer organization  

This engagement funds learning and proof more than profit.

---

## 8. MSP discovery without travel

Remote assessment kit:

- Floor/campus photos or simple sketches  
- ISP and firewall model/export  
- SSID list and who uses what  
- Critical Sunday services (stream, check-in, payment terminals)  
- Existing cameras/access vendors  
- Volunteer skill level  

Deliverables: prioritized findings, diagram, phased remote remediation plan, retainer option.

If on-site is mandatory: document as year-2+ or partner-assisted; do not eat travel costs in year 1.

## 10. Metrics that matter (year 1)

| Metric | Why |
|--------|-----|
| First paid SaaS customer live | Proof |
| First MSP retainer | Flywheel start |
| Qualified referrals | Reputation |
| Support load vs family capacity | Sustainability |
| Cost coverage | Stewardship |

Ignore: vanity follower counts, “leads” that never book calls.

---

## 11. Anti-patterns

- Promising on-site reno to win a deal  
- Building ChMS features before the first hosting/giving win  
- Undercutting forever without a subsidy policy  
- Aggressive “Christianese” marketing that alienates or feels cultish  
- Sunday heroics with no severity filter
