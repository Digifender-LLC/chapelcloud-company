## Sunday and 7-day support model

We offer **7-day support including Sunday service hours**. That is a promise of **faithful severity-tiered response**, not infinite unpaid labor.

### Rotation principles

1. One **primary on-call** for P1 windows (typically Sunday morning local-to-customer or agreed service times)
2. **Secondary** backup only for P1 escalation
3. P2/P3 do not interrupt worship unless they escalate to P1
4. After a heavy P1 Sunday, schedule recovery time — stewardship of family health is part of the mission
5. Retainers that include Sunday P1 must be priced to honor the cost of readiness

### Capacity guardrails

- Cap number of simultaneous Sunday-P1 retainers until a second qualified on-call exists
- If at capacity, waitlist or raise price / narrow SLA — do not silently overcommit
- Use runbooks so volunteers resolve common issues without calling us

Severity definitions: [../product/brief-msp.md](../product/brief-msp.md)
